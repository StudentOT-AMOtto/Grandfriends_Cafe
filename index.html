Five landmarks, five parts of life that moved online

Each place on this map stands for a set of things you once did by walking in through a door. Pick the one you want to work on today — the café for devices and staying safe, the post office for keeping in touch, the supermarket for food, the bus stop for getting about, the surgery for your health.     Come along on a Thursday

    Grandfriends Café runs at BEAT on Lea Farm Mount, Thursdays from 4 to 6pm. Bring your phone or tablet, or borrow one of ours. No appointment, no charge, and no question is too small.

    BEAT — Belonging, Empowering and Thriving · Lea Farm Mount, Leeds LS5 3PX · 0113 217 4651

    Volunteers: these guides pair with the Grandfriends Café resource pack. Each list of steps is already broken into chunks — take one chunk, tell them what you are about to do, show them, then hand the device back and let them do it.
